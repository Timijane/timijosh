Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TRIANGLE LEARN? +
TRIANGLE LEARN is a learning and career platform that connects learners, instructors, and employers.
Can I explore courses before creating an account? +
Yes. You can browse the public course catalogue and review available course information before registering. An account is required for enrolment and access to learner features.
How do I choose the right course? +
Start with your goal, experience level and the skills you want to develop. Review each course description, learning outcomes, requirements, duration and price before enrolling.
Can I learn at my own pace? +
Learning format and access conditions can vary by course. Check the course details for its schedule, lesson format, duration and any live-session requirements.
Are certificates available for every course? +
Certificate availability depends on the course. Where a certificate is offered, the course page will explain the completion requirements.
Who can teach on TRIANGLE LEARN? +
Qualified instructors can apply to join the platform. Instructor access and course publishing are subject to platform approval.
Can employers post jobs? +
Yes. Employers can create an account and use the platform's employer features to share opportunities and manage applications.

TRIANGLE LEARN is a learning and career platform built to make practical education more accessible. Discover courses, develop valuable skills, learn with guidance and take confident steps towards your future.
